Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to IPTI (Institute of Professional Training & Innovation). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, training programs, corporate services, application forms and other digital platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, disclose and protect your information when you visit our website, register for a course, apply for an internship, submit your resume, enquire about our services or interact with us.

By accessing our website or voluntarily providing your information, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information depending on how you interact with us:

A. Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • City, state or location
  • Age or date of birth, where required

B. Educational and Professional Information

  • Educational qualifications
  • College, university or institution details
  • Employment history and work experience
  • Skills, certifications and professional interests
  • Resume, CV, portfolio and supporting documents
  • Internship, placement or training application details

C. Training and Application Information

  • Course or program selected
  • Registration and enrollment details
  • Assessment, quiz and examination results
  • Attendance and participation records
  • Interview responses and feedback
  • Training progress and completion records

D. Corporate and Business Information

  • Company name and designation
  • Business email and contact details
  • Training requirements
  • Project details and service enquiries
  • Information voluntarily shared during business communication

E. Technical and Website Information

When you access our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Website pages visited
  • Access time, duration and interaction data
  • Cookies and similar technologies, where used

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information through:

  • Website registration and enquiry forms
  • Course and program applications
  • Internship and recruitment applications
  • Resume and document uploads
  • Online assessments and examinations
  • Contact forms and feedback forms
  • Emails, telephone calls and messaging platforms
  • Website cookies and analytics tools
  • Direct interactions with our representatives
  • Corporate training enquiries and service requests

We collect information that you voluntarily provide or that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or purpose.

4. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Processing course registrations and admissions
  • Evaluating training and internship applications
  • Reviewing resumes and professional qualifications
  • Providing training, educational and corporate services
  • Conducting assessments, interviews and examinations
  • Maintaining attendance, progress and completion records
  • Communicating schedules, updates and announcements
  • Responding to enquiries and providing support
  • Issuing certificates, letters and program-related documents, where applicable
  • Sharing relevant opportunities, internships or employment information
  • Improving our website, services, learning content and user experience
  • Maintaining security and preventing fraud or misuse
  • Complying with legal, regulatory and contractual obligations

We will not intentionally use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Resume and Application Data

When you submit a resume, CV, portfolio, application or other professional information, you authorize us to use that information for the purpose associated with your submission.

This may include:

  • Reviewing your profile and qualifications
  • Assessing your suitability for training, internship or professional opportunities
  • Contacting you regarding your application
  • Considering your profile for relevant opportunities
  • Sharing your profile with prospective employers or clients where relevant to an opportunity and permitted by applicable law

Submitting a resume does not guarantee admission, internship selection, employment, placement or interview opportunities.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information as a business.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Trainers, mentors and authorized program coordinators
  • Employers or organizations in connection with relevant opportunities, where appropriate
  • Service providers supporting website hosting, email, cloud storage, payments, communications or technical operations
  • Assessment, learning management or software platforms
  • Professional advisers where necessary
  • Government authorities, courts or regulators where required by law

We expect third-party service providers to handle information appropriately and maintain reasonable safeguards.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical, administrative and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Access controls and restricted permissions
  • Secure storage systems
  • Password protection and authentication
  • Security monitoring
  • Data backup practices
  • Appropriate safeguards when information is shared with service providers

However, no electronic storage system, website or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, provide services, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, meet contractual requirements or comply with applicable laws.

When personal information is no longer required, we may securely delete, anonymize or dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate record-retention requirements.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Ensure website functionality
  • Remember user preferences
  • Understand website usage
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Measure website traffic and engagement

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain website features may not function properly if essential cookies are disabled.

Where required, we will seek consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

10. Communication and Marketing

We may contact you through email, phone, SMS or other communication channels regarding:

  • Your registration or application
  • Course information and schedules
  • Program updates
  • Assessment or interview information
  • Services you have requested
  • Relevant professional opportunities
  • Promotional information, where permitted

You may opt out of promotional communications at any time. However, essential communications relating to a service, application, enrollment, payment or ongoing program may still be sent where necessary.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information that is no longer required or lawfully retained
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise concerns regarding the processing of your personal information
  • Submit a privacy-related grievance or request

Requests may require verification of your identity to protect your information.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, payment providers, learning portals or other external services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content or policies of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before sharing information with them.

13. Children's Privacy

Our programs and services are generally intended for individuals capable of registering for professional training and career-development services.

Where personal information of a child is collected, we will follow applicable legal requirements, including any required parental consent and safeguards.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data-handling practices, technology or applicable laws.

Any revised Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

15. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, you may contact us:

Company: Institute of Professional Training & Innovation
Email: privacy.enquiry@ipti.org
Subject: Privacy Request / Data Protection Enquiry

We will review and respond to privacy-related requests within the applicable timeframe under relevant law.

By using our website and submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the processing of information for the purposes described above, subject to applicable law and any consent required.
