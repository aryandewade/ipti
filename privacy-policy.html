Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Institute of Professional Training & Innovation (IPTI) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, enquiry forms, and learner portals.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information such as your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and academic or professional background when you submit an enquiry or enroll.
  • Enrollment, fee payment ledger, attendance, and certification records maintained for enrolled learners using their unique Student ID.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to program enquiries, provide counseling, share official syllabi, and communicate batch schedules.
  • To manage learner enrollment, fee receipts, assessments, and credential verification via Student ID.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

All enquiry and learner records are stored with restricted administrative access and password-protected authentication. Credentials issued by IPTI are verified solely through our official verification registry using your unique Student ID.

4. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data update requests, please contact IPTI Administration via our official contact channels on the main website.
