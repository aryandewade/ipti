COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining IPTI's professional programs.

What are the eligibility criteria for joining IPTI programs?
+

Our programs are open to Diploma holders, B.E./B.Tech graduates (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science, IT, Mechatronics), BCA/MCA graduates, as well as working professionals looking to upskill into core engineering domains.

How does the 100% placement assistance program work?
+

We provide full-spectrum career support: technical resume preparation, live mock interview drills with corporate hiring managers, GitHub/project portfolio reviews, and exclusive campus hiring drives through our network of 500+ engineering partners.

Are there weekend or flexible batches for working professionals?
+

Yes! We offer dedicated Weekend Hybrid Batches designed specifically for working professionals, featuring Saturday-Sunday practical sessions combined with self-paced LMS theory modules.

Is the certification IPTI Certified and industry recognized?
+

Yes. IPTI is a recognized professional training institute. Upon completing your capstone project and passing practical assessments, you receive an authorized certificate with an online verifiable credential ID.

Can I pay the course fee in installments (EMI)?
+

Yes, we provide 0% interest flexible monthly installment options (EMI) as well as merit scholarships for top academic achievers.
